Research

Research interests

High-dimensional inference & permutation tests

I study covariance matrix testing in high dimensions, with a focus on the asymptotic validity of permutation procedures based on the Ky Fan k-norm. My broader interests include empirical process theory and matrix analysis.

Network models & low-rank structure

I am interested in the statistical theory and algorithm design for generalized network models with low-rank latent structures, including estimation and inference, the asymptotic properties of estimators, and the development and convergence analysis of optimization algorithms.

Low-rank approximation & spectral methods

I am interested in low-rank approximation and spectral methods for understanding high-dimensional data and network structure, including the use of eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and singular values in statistical estimation and dimensionality reduction.

Nonlinear & functional dimensionality reduction

My undergraduate research explored PCA, functional PCA, t-SNE, and UMAP. I am interested in how these methods capture structure in high-dimensional data and how their performance differs across simulation settings.

Covariance Matrix Permutation Tests

Research Assistant

University of Wisconsin–Madison

Advisor: Professor Yinqiu He

Building on the work of Veitch, He, and Park (RACT), I study the null and permutation distributions of Ky Fan statistics, addressing the validity of permutation tests when two populations have the same mean and covariance but differ in distribution.

  • Extended asymptotic theory from fixed to growing dimensions.
  • Established permutation validity using empirical process methods and Stein’s method.
  • Evaluated finite-sample type I error control through simulations.
